## Headline shifts

- **Authoring agency rotates each drop** — R1 led with DoW + FBI (50% / 36%); R2 was a near-pure DoW video dump (81% DoW, 0% FBI); R3 swings to FBI + CIA (40% FBI, 25% CIA) and introduces ICA and USG.
- **Geography flips from CENTCOM to CONUS** — CENTCOM/Middle-East share: R1 33%, R2 50%, R3 0%. CONUS share climbs to 56% in R3 — the newest drop is a domestic-US story (Western US, Colorado Springs, Northeastern orbs).
- **Redaction collapses in R3** — Redacted share: R1 66%, R2 84%, R3 17%. R3 also debuts the 'Featured' flag with 10 hero records — a more curated, less-redacted presentation.
- **Asset mix: documents → video → documents** — PDF share: R1 73%, R2 9%, R3 74%. R2 was 80% video (raw DVIDS clips); R3 returns to a paper-heavy mix with a wave of FBI still images.
- **R3 reaches deeper into the 1950s-60s** — Mid-century incidents jump in R3: 1950s 18% (R1 3%), 1960s 15% (R1 6%) — driven by CIA Cold-War files and the Mercury/Gemini NASA expansion.
- **Shape vocabulary returns in R3** — R2's raw clips carried almost no descriptive language; R3 restores rich morphology — sphere/orb 21%, disc 24%, circular 25% — closer to R1's witness-narrative texture (sphere/orb 13%).
